A compressed landing opens obliquely into a shared work gallery. The rail buffer and braced geology explain the upstream material route; the mill, bench, forge hood and remaining roof carry separate, believable jobs. Low foreground edges retain orbit-camera visibility.

Remaining review points: exact interactive artwork bindings, tighter material transitions and the amount of open sanctuary floor. The small bank/altar/plaque envelopes are not implemented service replacements.

The dry route rises outside the encounter envelope. The pump connects to an overhead supply; the paired return descends only over the water. Supports, quiet source vent grates and a lowered ash trench explain safe passage and industrial danger. The Imp's open floor stays attached to that system.
