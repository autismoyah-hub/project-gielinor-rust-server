# Direction B: comprehensive Floor One

The original Tower is a technical foundation, not a fixed creative specification. Agent 5 is authorized to make ordinary creative decisions and implement the complete first chapter without waiting for routine approvals.

The experience must have a recognizable entry, discovery/preparation, escalation, substantive demi-boss, phased final boss, meaningful resolution, durable rewards, recovery and repeatability. Select mechanics that reinforce one another and favor mastery over repetitive grinding.

Use readable terrain, landmarks and supported revision-239.1 assets. Render early to judge geometry, collision, camera visibility and telegraphs. Layout and encounter design must be evaluated in the actual native client.

The initial architectural recommendation is a checkpoint ascent using a small set of mutually supporting activities. Its exact identity, route, encounters and completion model will be authored after the first rendered baseline. This recommendation does not limit the mandate to an initial slice; continue through full Floor One acceptance.

The unauthored upstairs stub is not an ending. Provide a complete Floor One resolution independently of future floors.
