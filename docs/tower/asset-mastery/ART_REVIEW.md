# Visual assessment and retained failures

All private reconstructions remain unapproved. Source native views, coarse
reconstruction views and later proposals are compared at identical cameras.
These are target-derived private studies, never original Tower content.

| Case | Actionable visual deficiency |
|---|---|
| Fire Giant | Section lobes distort the chest/shoulder transitions; head and jaw structure lack the source's deliberate planes. Color placement alone does not repair anatomy. |
| Graardor | Broad silhouette overlap conceals missing facial structure and armor emblems. Rounded partitions do not reproduce the layered equipment construction. |
| K'ril | Horn, weapon and muscle regions remain fragmented; narrow source features need intentional curved constructions and connected anatomical landmarks. |
| Xarpus | Coarse wing partitions lose the membrane's leading/trailing edges and deliberate negative space. Source-control deformation is not new choreography. |
| Verzik | Ornate head, fabric and equipment construction merge into simple masses. Later forms require deliberate appendage structure, material boundaries and independent phase choreography. |
| Akkha | The frozen 50-vertex candidate collapses anatomy and equipment into one mass. Dominant-bone static partitioning recovers some body structure but still distorts the aura/equipment into large fused shapes. It is not faithful. Weighted animation remains unsupported. |

After the examination, an additional static proposal uses coarse area-weighted
native color brushes. For weighted sources it partitions observations by dominant
bone before constructing new section topology; no original weighted frames or
source mesh topology are transferred. This is implemented in
adaptive_reconstruction.py and stays private when exercised on protected inputs.

Eleven form proposals improve mean foreground-union RGB error in seven forms and
regress four. Graardor, K'ril and Verzik phases 1 and 3 regress. The slight Xarpus
terminal improvement is small. Akkha improves color error substantially while
still looking wrong: this is a useful counterexample to numerical acceptance.
No proposal is automatically promoted based on mean color error or silhouette.
The separate v2 motion evidence retains its v2 materials; v3 has static evidence
only. All candidates, including regressions, remain available locally.

Original bosses demonstrate reusable construction and conversion, not final art.
The biped designs still share visible family proportions. Fine hands, faces,
equipment construction, silhouette hierarchy and forceful anticipation need
further art review. Foot-contact/body-support proxy observations are retained in
the public motion diagnostics; they are not hidden by the scene presentation.

Floor v5 resolves technical foundation occlusion and inflated tile shading, but
does not yet meet the requested professional environmental standard. Paving is
still too regular, rock faces too simple, several areas too sparse, water too
uniform and material transitions too broad. The bell/workshop/water sequence is
readable, but the foundry needs more convincing industrial infrastructure and
visual hierarchy before approval. It remains a complete original offline study,
with two player views per area and whole-floor evidence, not a production map.

For ATELIER, the next concrete improvements are curated connected anatomical and
equipment partitions, explicit facial/armor construction, planar/annular effect
recognition instead of coarse solids, multi-angle material review, and correction
of native stance drift/unsupported feet. Environment production should refine
ground construction, geological mass and industrial purpose before adding prop
density. These are handoff items, not an authorization to replace the live map.
