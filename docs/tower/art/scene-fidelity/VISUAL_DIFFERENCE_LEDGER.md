## Density evidence, not a similarity score

Within a25×25 plane0 survey box around the selected industrial camera focus, the genuine map has225 placement records, including65 wall6098,49 interior-wall6145 and20 hanging-lantern3474 records. The dungeon and river boxes have537 and409 records, including many low ground decorations and boundary pieces. These counts are not target budgets: original wall modules often occupy one tile, custom walls span two, shapes/categories differ, and many source records are hidden or merely define boundaries.

The candidate has93 authored placements from38 used asset families inside a compact connected suite and its immediate boundary. It uses a58-model original library including six new detail families; native terrain is counted separately. The result is judged from actual images and functional grouping, not by optimizing raw object counts or a similarity percentage.

## Review decision still required

Retain the candidate as a focused scene study. Do not promote it or propagate these choices across Floor One until the user has reviewed the artistic result. Main remaining questions are wall surface/normal treatment, richer original material detail, restrained terrain strata and whether the current occupation density and palette meet the desired OSRS standard.
