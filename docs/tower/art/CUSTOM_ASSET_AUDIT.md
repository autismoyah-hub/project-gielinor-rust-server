## Missing families

- Bonded masonry corners, finished wall ends, load-bearing buttresses, integrated retaining banks and thresholds.
- Worker beds, stools, coat/tool hooks, meal/ledger station, personal stores and maintenance clutter for inhabited sanctuary.
- Headgate and sluice controls, spill apron, intake screen, drainage culvert, believable bridge abutments and fishing landings.
- Connected mine transport/stock handling, sorting piles, timber supports and wall-attached extraction scars.
- Foundry containment ribs, proper casting spout, crane load path and a completion portal distinct from everyday machinery.
- Continuous original floor/bedrock surfaces, placed repairs, damp and ash transitions; terrain height/collision requirements documented separately.

## Decisions and integration boundary

Retain does not imply artistic approval. Refinements retain stable original custom IDs; replacements are versioned definitions under the same role IDs where suitable. New families allocate IDs after model 1100067/object 63067 and reject duplicates. All current object definitions are nonblocking offline proposals. Collision, action bindings, resource ownership, combat safe space, heights/planes, animation and completion persistence remain future integration work. No current gameplay changes are authorized by this art audit.
