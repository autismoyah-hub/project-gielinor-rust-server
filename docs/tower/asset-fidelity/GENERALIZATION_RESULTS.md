# Frozen generalization evaluation

Nine selectors were declared before authoring tuning. Frozen v1 records exact
tool/catalog/native-executable hashes before inspection and descriptor fitting.
The exact Python fixture is archived under
`tools/tower/asset-workbench/fixtures/frozen-descriptor-v1-python.zip`; the old
native executable and protected reference fixtures remain private. Source/tool
identity details are in FROZEN_V1_TOOL_HASHES.json. The published later adapter
adds submitted-surface diagnostics; it is not represented as the old binary.

No held-out raw-model payload overlaps the 33 base training variants or two
supplementary worn inspections. The experiment is **descriptor-driven fitting**,
not blind class prediction: target bounds, profiles and palette descriptors are
available to a frozen owned archetype generator. Semantic class selection is
explicit. Technical B preservation passes all nine assets separately, including
the two C-unsupported categories; that is not authoring success.

Barrel/crate volume transfers most readily, but plank/stave construction and
material detail remain weak. The wall slab misses masonry structure; the straight
blade archetype cannot express scimitar curvature. Wolf anatomy/coat/gait remain
wrong. Full helmets and winged creatures lack supported frozen archetypes.
No held-out asset is artistically accepted.

The anvil exposed a general descriptor flaw: invisible helper faces inflate full
decoded bounds. Orientation-only correction leaves its score unchanged at 0.341.
A new native-submitted-surface descriptor improves the same development asset to
0.752. The failed v1 result is preserved and this correction is **not** counted
as a new held-out pass. No fresh sealed cohort has yet established general
artistic transfer after this change. Further tools should address semantic
construction, anatomy and curved equipment before such a claim.
