# Original Tower asset review

Three genuinely new owned-component designs test art-direction transfer. They
are **probes**, not proof that earlier artistic milestones have passed. All remain
unapproved and outside gameplay. Their recipes, geometry, native models, authored
actions and standalone native renders are published under
`art/tower/asset-workbench-originals/`; protected scenes containing genuine cache
assets remain local. No original mesh, original frame payload or direct reference
reconstruction is included in the publishable asset set.

| Design | New model / object | Geometry | Private genuine comparison |

The tensioner's saddle uses split masonry piers, a bronze roller, cable guide and
load-bearing stays. The twin-throat sluice governor has unequal chambers, an
equalizer, handwheel, drains and a ceramic pressure flag. The kilnback rummager
has six articulated legs, a vented ceramic carapace and copper mouthparts.
Its five original actions pass 447 exact native pose checks. New NPC identity
60024, sequence range 45032–45039, skeleton range 32032–32039 and frame-group
range 40032–40039 are allocated locally; no live NPC overlay was installed.

The initial probes' blue metal and buried shell vents were retained as a prior
version. The second version uses less saturated iron and visible vents. Actual
shared native scenes use an 80-unit separation, common 64/768 lighting, one camera
and unchanged native dimensions. Close and gameplay views are provided. These
scenes answer a review question; they do not imply that the assets naturally
belong in the world. Material placement, shape hierarchy, character and motion
still require artistic judgment.

All three original packages also load through the native object-definition reader;
all 15 registered native views exactly match their standalone views. This validates
isolated object registration, not live NPC or gameplay integration.
