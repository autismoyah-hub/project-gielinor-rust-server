## Executed feasibility audit

The isolated [design_audit](../../../../tools/tower/offline/export/src/bin/design_audit.rs) loads real cache definitions and terrain, constructs each detached scene, applies real location clipping and searches with normal movement. It checks every required waypoint, each operable object's cardinal access, all four fish spots, clear boss spawn footprints, and 1,512 hazard escape cases drawn from the current phase patterns. Scene route illustrations use its actual clipped paths, not straight lines through walls.

Every candidate passes with zero issues. Counts and paths are in the manifest; full logs remain local. Initial B/C waypoints were on occupied scenery; these failed audits are retained, and corrected waypoint/camera positions were rechecked. No gameplay rule was relaxed.

This does **not** validate moving NPCs, actual combat, telegraph timing, rescue alternatives, restoration states, account migration, interface visibility or native scene lifecycle. Camera ground vertices were checked and views inspected, but the offline look-at camera is not the live viewport controller. Major approval must precede native implementation and normal-action acceptance.
